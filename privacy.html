The Footy App

Privacy policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

In short: The Footy App has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it collects no personal information.

Who makes the app

The Footy App is made by GARTH D MC LAUGHLIN. You can contact me at thefootyappemail@gmail.com.

What the app collects

The app itself collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no personal information. I don't receive or store any information about you or your device.

The app doesn't ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

What the app stores on your device

The only thing the app saves is the last team you selected in the Fixture, so it can offer it again next time. It is kept on your device and never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Where the football data comes from

The app downloads public football data (ladder, fixture and scores) from the Squiggle API (api.squiggle.com.au, run by squiggle.com.au) over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Like any request on the internet, each request tells Squiggle your device's IP address, along with a request header that identifies the app. The app sends nothing else about you, and I don't receive this information. Squiggle is an independent service with its own practices.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
